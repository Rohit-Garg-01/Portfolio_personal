About Me

I'm a creative designer with a sharp eye for visuals and a passion for storytelling. My work spans poster design, AI-generated art, video editing, social media content, and brand identity.

As a Social Media Executive Volunteer for CIA Conference (CIACON 2026) — India's premier cybersecurity conference — I design promotional campaigns, create engaging reels, and manage the conference's digital presence.

I also create content for college societies like The Security Lens Insights, Nexera AI, and The Hype Crew — blending creativity with impact.

Beyond design, I'm a passionate photographer — capturing moments through my lens, focusing on street, portrait, and event photography.
